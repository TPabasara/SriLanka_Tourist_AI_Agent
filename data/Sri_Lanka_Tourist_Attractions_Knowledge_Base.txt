SRI LANKA TOURIST ATTRACTIONS - NATIONAL KNOWLEDGE BASE
=======================================================
Prepared: 8 November 2026 | Plain text | Purpose: Tourist-guide Agent / vector database

SCOPE AND ACCURACY
------------------
This is a broad national directory of major and notable visitor attractions across all nine provinces and all 25 districts. Sri Lanka has thousands of small local shrines, beaches, waterfalls, viewpoints and community attractions; no finite public register establishes every attraction. This file must not be represented as literally containing every place in the country.
Opening hours, fees, permits, ferry schedules, road access and seasonal restrictions change. Where a current official amount or schedule has not been verified, the record says to confirm it. Do not invent prices, hours, addresses, coordinates or wildlife sightings.
UNESCO 'Yes' means the named property is inscribed on the World Heritage List. Nearby sites are not automatically UNESCO properties. Tentative List status is not inscription.
Religious sites: modest clothing, footwear removal where requested, respect rituals and obey photography restrictions. Wildlife: keep distance, do not feed animals and use authorised operators. Nature: check weather, surf, currents, flash-flood risk and trail status.

ATTRACTION 001: Gangaramaya Temple
Location: Colombo 2
City: Colombo
District: Western
Province: Multi-province / verify district boundary
Category: Buddhist temple and museum
UNESCO World Heritage Site: No
Overview and Description: A major Colombo Buddhist institute combining Sri Lankan, Thai, Indian and Chinese architectural influences. Its complex includes shrines, a library and eclectic museum collections.
Historical and Cultural Significance: Founded in the late nineteenth century under Hikkaduwe Sri Sumangala Nayaka Thera. The annual Nawam Maha Perahera is a major Colombo religious procession.
Main Attractions and Activities: Explore shrine buildings, Buddha images, antiques and museum displays; combine with nearby Seema Malaka.
Practical Visitor Information - Location and Access: Central Colombo; taxi, tuk-tuk and bus access.
Operating Hours: Hours vary by section and ceremony; confirm locally.
Entry Fees (Foreign): Foreign: museum charge may apply; verify. Temple worship access may differ.
Entry Fees (Local): Local: worship access generally available; verify museum tariff.
Visitor Guidelines and Tips: Cover shoulders and knees; remove shoes and hats where requested; behave quietly. Daytime suits museum visits; festival dates vary.

UNESCO WORLD HERITAGE REFERENCE
================================
UNESCO lists eight Sri Lankan World Heritage properties:
1. Sacred City of Anuradhapura (1982) - North Central Province.
2. Ancient City of Polonnaruwa (1982) - North Central Province.
3. Ancient City of Sigiriya (1982) - Central Province, Matale District.
4. Old Town of Galle and its Fortifications (1988) - Southern Province.
5. Sacred City of Kandy (1988) - Central Province.
6. Sinharaja Forest Reserve (1988) - Sabaragamuwa / Southern region.
7. Rangiri Dambulla Cave Temple (1991) - Central Province, Matale District.
8. Central Highlands of Sri Lanka (2010) - includes Peak Wilderness, Horton Plains and Knuckles components.

AGENT RESPONSE RULES
====================
- Disambiguate attraction names and return province, district and nearest town.
- Do not say a place is open now unless a current authoritative schedule has been checked.
- Do not quote old blog prices as current fees.
- For national parks, separate entrance, service, vehicle, guide and safari-vehicle charges.
- For religious places, distinguish free worship access from paid museums or special areas.
- For beaches and waterfalls, include weather, surf, current, flash-flood and cliff-edge safety.
- Never promise wildlife sightings or encourage feeding, touching, chasing or baiting animals.
- For islands and remote parks, verify ferry, road, permit and return-trip arrangements.
- When uncertain, say what must be confirmed and identify the responsible authority.
- Never fabricate a street address, GPS coordinate, telephone number, opening hour, fee or booking link.
